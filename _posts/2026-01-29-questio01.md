## Why this question matters


Closely related species often look similar, share ecological niches, and may even interbreed under certain conditions—yet they are biologically distinct. These differences can show up as:

Adaptations to different environments

Differences in metabolism or behavior

Disease resistance or susceptibility

Observable traits like size, color, or growth rate

At the molecular level, these differences arise from genetic variation: changes in DNA sequence that accumulate over evolutionary time.
