As a researcher and computational biologist, I’ve often fallen into the trap of running a pipeline without fully understanding the biology behind it. Research should begin with a question, and I’ve come to believe that running a pipeline well requires a clear understanding of the biological problem it is meant to answer, not just the code that executes it. 

**Thinking in Genomes** is a question-driven blog about genomics, evolution, and metagenomics.

Each post starts with a biological question and works through:
- the biological reasoning
- the type of data needed
- the appropriate class of analysis
- how to interpret results responsibly

My hope is that this blog helps move readers from simply running pipelines to truly thinking in genomes.
